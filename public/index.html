Space Mission Design Game

Challenge: design, fly and protect a space mission

Designing a space mission means weighing competing demands: objectives, spacecraft design, instruments, launch vehicles, budgets, power, mass, communications and orbital constraints. Project Ysoriel turns those abstract trade-offs into two connected simulations, so you can make engineering decisions, manage limited resources and see how each choice shapes mission success.

MODULE 1 · DESIGN

Mission Design Studio

Take a mission from concept to operations. Build the spacecraft, then fly it.

  • Three missions: Earth, Moon and Mars
  • Launch vehicle, bus, propulsion, power, comms and instruments
  • Live budget, mass, power, delta-v and data checks
  • Animated flight with a graded debrief
MODULE 2 · OPERATE

Orbital Guardian

Run a debris-removal tug in a crowded orbit and manage fuel, risk and reputation.

  • Five catalogued debris targets with different risk and cost
  • Plan approaches, spend fuel and time your capture
  • Upgrade your tug with earned credits
  • Orbit, risk and altitude views
