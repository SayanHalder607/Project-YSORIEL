    Project Ysoriel v1.1 · Educational simulation. Uses the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation, inverse-distance solar flux and simplified reliability models; values are illustrative, not real mission guidance.

    How to play

    1. Pick a mission. It sets your budget, destination, Δv need and science goal.
    2. Design the spacecraft: launch vehicle, bus, propulsion, power, communications and instruments.
    3. Balance the trade-offs. Mass, power, Δv, data downlink and cost all interact. Every requirement must pass to launch.
    4. Simulate. Launch, insertion, commissioning and six months of science operations are rolled against reliability and margins.
    5. Learn and improve. Healthy margins, balanced data and budget reserve earn a higher score.
